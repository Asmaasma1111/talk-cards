# Sounds in مكتبتي

Every sound here is a real recording released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No credit is required; it is given here anyway.
The files were trimmed, faded, levelled and saved as AAC (.m4a) for the iPad. The glass chime when a shelf
door opens, the celebration fanfare and the confetti are made in code (library.js), not recordings.
