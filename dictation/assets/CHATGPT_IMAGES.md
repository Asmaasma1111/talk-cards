# كرّاس الإملاء: the pictures to make in ChatGPT

Paste the STYLE LOCK first in a new ChatGPT chat. Then paste one numbered prompt at a time and save each picture into
`dictation/assets/` under its exact file name. Until a picture exists, the app shows a soft box with the file name, so
everything already works without them.

Three sizes are used, because they are the sizes ChatGPT makes:
- square: 1024 x 1024
- portrait: 1024 x 1536
- landscape: 1536 x 1024

---

## STYLE LOCK (paste this first)

> I am designing a clean, beautiful iPad app for an 8-year-old girl in Saudi Arabia that teaches Arabic spelling through
> an Algerian school copybook. Every image I ask for in this chat must share one style: soft, rounded, flat 2D
> illustration with gentle shading, like a premium children's app; calm, never noisy or cluttered; main colours soft pink,
> warm yellow and fresh green, with a clear blue for ink; paper and card that feel real, with a faint natural texture.
> No text, no letters, no numbers and no words anywhere in any image unless I ask for them. Transparent background unless
> I say otherwise. One subject per image, centred, with a little empty space around it. High resolution. Please keep
> exactly this style for every image I ask for next.
