# مسرح المسائل pictures

Since 8 Nov 2026 every picture in the story stage is drawn in ChatGPT (Khaled's account), soft 3D clay style,
from scratch (her request: "use ChatGPT to render all the images for this one entirely").

- things/ (people, animals, objects) and packs/ (the 10, 100 and 1000 containers): cut from five sprite sheets by
  scripts/theatre_sheets.py (200 px WebP). The sheets themselves stay local in content/theatre/sheets/ (off GitHub).
- stage/board.webp (landscape) and stage/board-tall.webp (upright iPad): the clay stage behind the things.

The prompts are in CHATGPT_IMAGES.md. The earlier Fluent Emoji icons (Microsoft, MIT licence) are no longer used;
scripts/theatre_icons.py would bring them back and overwrite the clay pictures, so don't run it.
